Meet the neighbors

Small shells. Big stories.

From sun-baked islands to scrubby grasslands, tortoises have adapted to an amazing range of habitats.

🌋Island giant

Galápagos tortoise

The largest living tortoise. Its long neck and distinctive shell shape help it reach plants across the Galápagos Islands.

Pacific Ocean·Up to 5 ft long
🌿Forest dweller

Aldabra giant tortoise

One of the biggest tortoises on Earth, this gentle grazer lives on the Aldabra Atoll in the Seychelles.

Indian Ocean·Island ecosystem
☀️Desert favorite

Sulcata tortoise

Also called the African spurred tortoise, it is well suited to hot, dry places and digs burrows to escape the heat.

Sub-Saharan Africa·Powerful digger
🌼Colorful shell

Indian star tortoise

Its beautifully patterned shell has star-like markings. This shy tortoise is found in dry scrub and grassland.

India & Sri Lanka·Distinctive pattern
🍂Forest wanderer

Red-footed tortoise

Bright scales on its legs make this South American species easy to recognize. It enjoys a varied diet of plants and fruit.

South America·Omnivorous
🪨Tiny tortoise

One of the world's smallest tortoises, it blends into rocky South African terrain and shelters in small crevices.

South Africa·Just a few inches
